## Charity Support System Concept

### 1. Purpose of the System

The system will help the charity organization manage and publish support projects for poor and homeless people who need help. The main goal is to allow donors and volunteers to support the charity’s mission without meeting the beneficiaries directly.

The organization will be responsible for identifying the people in need, verifying their situation, collecting support, and delivering help such as houses, mattresses, home tools, food, clothes, and other basic needs.

---

## 2. Main Users

### Public Visitor

A person who visits the website to learn about the charity.

### Donor

A person who wants to support with money, materials, or other resources.

### Volunteer

A person who wants to offer time, skills, labor, or professional help.

### Admin / Charity Staff

The internal team that manages beneficiaries, projects, donations, volunteers, and reports.

### Beneficiary

The poor or homeless person/family being supported.
They do not directly interact with donors through the platform.

## 4. Important Workflow

### Beneficiary Support Flow

1. Charity staff identifies a homeless/poor family.
2. Staff records their information in the system.
3. Staff verifies the case.
4. Admin creates a support project.
5. Project is published on the website.
6. Donors or volunteers support the project.
7. Charity collects the support.
8. Charity delivers the help to the beneficiary.
9. Admin updates project progress.
10. Completed project is shown as an impact story.

---

## 5. Volunteer Flow

1. Visitor opens the volunteer page.
2. They fill in:

   * Name
   * Phone/email
   * Address
   * Skills
   * Type of support
   * Availability
3. Admin receives the application.
4. Admin reviews and approves.
5. Volunteer is assigned to a project or activity.
6. Admin tracks volunteer contribution.

---

## 6. Donor Flow

1. Donor visits the website.
2. Donor chooses a project or general donation.
3. Donor gives support.
4. Charity receives the support.
5. Admin records the donation.
6. Charity uses the support for the beneficiary.
7. Donor can see public progress updates.

Important:
**Donors will not contact beneficiaries directly. The charity organization remains the trusted bridge between donors and beneficiaries.**
